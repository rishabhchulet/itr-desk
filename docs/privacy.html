Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

What ITR Desk is

ITR Desk is a browser extension that helps chartered accountants and tax practitioners work faster on the Income Tax e-Filing portal (eportal.incometax.gov.in). It is operated by an independent developer in India (the "Operator") and is not affiliated with the Income Tax Department or the Government of India.

Data the extension stores

  • Client records you enter: name, PAN, date of birth or incorporation, tags and free-text notes.
  • Notice records you enter (Pro): section, DIN/reference, due date, status and notes.
  • Your settings (keep-alive, autofill, download folder).
  • Your licence key and its activation status (Pro).

All of this is stored locally in your browser's extension storage on your device. The Operator has no server that receives it and cannot see it. The extension never asks for, reads or stores portal passwords or OTPs.

Data that leaves your device

  • Licence checks (Pro only): activation sends your licence key and a short label to Dodo Payments (for example "ITR Desk on Windows" in Chrome/Edge, or "ITR Desk" in Firefox). Validation sends the licence key and activation instance ID. Results are cached for seven days, with up to fourteen days of offline grace. See Dodo Payments' privacy policy.
  • Autofill: the active client's PAN is placed in the portal's User ID field. The portal and its scripts can read that field before you submit. The extension never submits your login.
  • Purchases: payments are handled entirely by Dodo Payments as merchant of record. The Operator receives your email address and order details from Dodo to provide support; card details never reach the Operator.

Client records are not uploaded to the Operator. Apart from the PAN you autofill into the portal, the extension does not send client data or portal content to another service. It does not use analytics or advertising SDKs.

Permissions explained

  • storage – to keep your client list and settings locally.
  • sidePanel (Chrome/Edge) or Firefox's sidebar interface – to show ITR Desk next to the portal.
  • downloads – to rename portal files into a client folder in Chrome/Edge and to export CSV files on request. Firefox cannot automatically sort portal downloads; it uses your normal download folder. The extension does not read file contents.
  • alarms – to schedule the periodic licence re-check.
  • Host access to eportal.incometax.gov.in – to show the badge, fill the User ID box and keep the session alive. The extension does not run on any other site.
  • Host access to live.dodopayments.com (Firefox) – licence activation and validation, without access to payment-card details.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it. Use "Delete all local data" in Settings or uninstall the extension to remove everything. Order records held by Dodo Payments are retained according to their policy and applicable tax law.

Children

ITR Desk is a professional tool and is not directed at children under 18.
