Activate in three steps

  1. Open ITR Desk

    Click the ITR Desk icon in Chrome's toolbar (or press Alt+Shift+I) to open the side panel.

  2. Settings → Licence

    Go to the Settings tab, scroll to Licence, and paste the key from the email.

  3. Activate

    Click Activate. The badge in the header switches to Pro and the limits lift immediately.
