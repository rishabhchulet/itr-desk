## Description
Built for chartered accountants, tax practitioners and accounts teams who log in to the Income Tax e-Filing portal for many clients every day.

WHAT IT DOES
• Client switcher – keep your client list (name, PAN, DOB, notes) in a side panel. One click makes a client active.
• PAN autofill – the active client's PAN is filled into the User ID box on the login page. You type the password and OTP as usual.
• Session keep-alive – gentle activity pings and automatic dismissal of the "session about to expire" prompt while the portal tab is open.
• Downloads that file themselves – 26AS, AIS, ITR-V, intimations and notices downloaded from the portal are saved to Downloads/ITR Desk/<Client>/ automatically.
• Due-date strip – advance tax, TDS deposits and returns, Form 16/16A, tax audit and ITR deadlines, always showing what is next.

PRO (₹999/year)
• Unlimited clients, tags and filters
• Notice & demand tracker – log 143(1), 139(9), 148 and demand notices per client with response due dates
• Downloads split by client and assessment year
• CSV import/export of your client list

PRIVACY
Everything is stored locally in your browser. No account, no sync server, no analytics. The extension never asks for or stores portal passwords or OTPs. The only network call is the licence check for Pro users.

ITR Desk only runs on eportal.incometax.gov.in. It is an independent tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Income Tax Department or the Government of India.

## Single-purpose statement (for the review form)
Helps tax practitioners manage multiple clients on the Income Tax e-Filing portal: switch the active client, autofill the PAN as User ID, keep the session alive, organise portal downloads per client, and show compliance due dates.

## Permission justifications
- storage: save client list and settings locally.
- sidePanel: show the client switcher next to the portal.
- downloads: rename portal downloads into a per-client folder (onDeterminingFilename). File contents are not read.
- alarms: schedule the periodic licence re-check.
- host permission eportal.incometax.gov.in: inject the badge, fill the User ID field and keep the session alive. No other sites.
- Remote code: none. All code is packaged.
