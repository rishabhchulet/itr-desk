FAQ

Does it store passwords?

No. It stores the client name, PAN, date of birth and your notes, locally in your browser profile. Passwords and OTPs are always typed by you.

Does anything leave my computer?

Only the licence check, which sends your licence key to Dodo Payments to confirm it is valid. No client data is transmitted anywhere.

Is this affiliated with the Income Tax Department?

No. ITR Desk is an independent productivity tool and is not endorsed by or affiliated with the Income Tax Department or the Government of India.

Does it automate filing or bypass anything?

No. It fills a text box, keeps your session from idling out, and organises files. Every submission on the portal is done by you.
